## The Story

I've been a runner my whole life, but I never thought of myself as a runner. I ran local 5k races on weekends, finishing top 20 with a PR around 17 minutes. Then I got an idea: if I can run sub 5:45 per mile for 3 miles, I should easily be able to run 6:45 per mile for a marathon. How hard could it be?

My first marathon started at a blazing sub-three pace. Until mile 18. The wall. I finished in 4 hours, the hardest way possible.

After that failure, it was on. Project Sub Three was born.

I tried 11 more times. All failures. During this time I was building miCoach at Adidas, working alongside some of the top running coaches in the world. I was literally helping build the coaching tools while trying to coach myself to a sub-three marathon.

On attempt #12, after a full year of dedicated focus, I broke through. 2 hours, 58 minutes.

I believe what I learned across those 12 attempts can help other runners get there in fewer tries.

## The Product Concept

**What:** An AI training companion for runners chasing sub-three (or any ambitious marathon goal). Not a training plan generator. A companion that knows YOU: your history, your readiness, your tendencies, your weak spots.

**How it's different:** Most running apps give you a plan and leave you alone. Project Sub Three gives you context. Where are you right now? What should you focus on this week? What should you expect at mile 18 based on how you've been training? It's the coaching conversation I wish I'd had during attempts 1 through 11.

**Platform:** Web-first (also releasable as a mobile app). AI backend for personalization and coaching intelligence.

**Revenue model:** The app is free. Revenue comes from the brand. "Project Sub Three" is a badge runners want to wear. T-shirts, gear, merch for the community of runners chasing the goal. The tech builds the audience, the brand monetizes it.

**The real gem:** The "Project Sub Three" identity. There's a core runner out there, the one who's fast enough to dream about it but hasn't done it yet, who wants to wear this as a badge. The community of chasers is the product. The AI companion is what earns their trust.

- Web app (React or similar), designed mobile-first for responsive use
- AI backend (Claude API or similar) for the coaching companion
- User context model: training history, race goals, readiness signals, personal tendencies
